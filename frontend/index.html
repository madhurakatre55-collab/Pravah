WHY PRAVAH

Terrain that breaks ordinary logistics tools

Steep gradients, monsoon landslides and thin road networks make the North East one of the hardest regions in India to keep moving. PRAVAH was built for exactly that terrain.

01

Frequent road cut-offs

Landslides and flash floods routinely sever single-road districts for days at a time.

02

Sparse connectivity data

Field offices report status by phone; there is no shared, real-time picture.

03

Delayed emergency response

Medical and relief convoys lose critical hours finding a passable route.

04

Unpredictable weather

Rainfall intensity varies sharply across short distances in hill terrain.
